3D Force Equilibrium

Enter the X, Y and Z components of each force. The calculator adds all components, finds the resultant and gives the force required to balance the system.

1. Equilibrium condition

ΣFx = 0   ΣFy = 0   ΣFz = 0
R = √[(ΣFx)² + (ΣFy)² + (ΣFz)²]

A force is entered by its Cartesian components. Positive and negative signs indicate direction along each axis.

2. Enter forces

Example: a force of 10 N along +X is entered as Fx = 10, Fy = 0, Fz = 0. Use negative values for the opposite direction.
