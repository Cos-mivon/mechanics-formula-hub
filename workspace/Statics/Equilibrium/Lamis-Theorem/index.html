Lami's Theorem

F₁ / sin α = F₂ / sin β = F₃ / sin γ

For three coplanar, concurrent and non-collinear forces in equilibrium:

  • F₁, F₂ and F₃ are the force magnitudes.
  • α, β and γ are the opposite angles in the triangle of forces.
α + β + γ = 180°

The angles in the concurrent-force diagram are supplementary to the corresponding triangle angles.
