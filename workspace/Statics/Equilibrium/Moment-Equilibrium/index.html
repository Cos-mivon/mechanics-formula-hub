Moment Formulas

M = F × d

Moment = Force × Perpendicular distance

MO = xFy − yFx

Where:

  • x, y = Coordinates of the force application point.
  • Fx, Fy = Horizontal and vertical force components.
  • M = Moment about reference point O.
ΣMO = 0

Anticlockwise moment = Positive (+)
Clockwise moment = Negative (−)

All coordinates are measured from the fixed origin O. Angles are measured anticlockwise from the positive X-axis.
